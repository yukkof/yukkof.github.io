常见问题

Questions

试用要填信用卡吗？Do I need a card for the trial?

不需要。注册即得 14 天专业版，全部功能开放，到期自动回落免费版，不会扣款，也不会自动续费。

No. Registering gives you 14 days of Pro with every feature unlocked. When it ends the account simply reverts to Free — no charge, no auto-renewal.

数据存在哪里？换电脑会丢吗？Where is my data? Will it survive a new computer?

默认存在你浏览器的本地存储，不上传服务器。所以换电脑、换浏览器、清缓存前请先导出备份（JSON），或配置 WebDAV 同步。这一点在隐私政策和服务条款里都写明了。

In your browser's local storage by default — nothing is uploaded. Before switching computers or clearing browser data, export a JSON backup or set up WebDAV sync. This is spelled out in our Privacy Policy and Terms.

一次买断和年付有什么区别？Lifetime vs yearly — what's the difference?

功能完全一样。年付每年续费，买断一次付清、含 3 年更新，3 年后停止收新功能但仍可继续使用。用满两年半，买断就更划算。

Identical features. Yearly renews each year; lifetime is a single payment including 3 years of updates, after which you keep what you have but stop receiving new features. Past about two and a half years, lifetime works out cheaper.

可以退款吗？Can I get a refund?

可以。购买后 14 天内，无需理由，全额退款，退回原支付渠道。发邮件到 495509885@qq.com 并附上订单号即可。

Yes. Full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked, back to the original payment method. Email 495509885@qq.com with your order number.

一个授权码能用几台设备？How many devices per licence?

授权码绑定你本人，不限你自己拥有的设备数量。但不能分享给别人，也不能转卖。

A key is bound to you personally and covers any devices you own or control. It may not be shared or resold.

大陆用户怎么付？能开发票吗？How do I pay from mainland China? Can I get an invoice?

可通过邮件下单，用微信或支付宝转账，人工核发授权码。个人开发者无法开具增值税专用发票，可提供收款凭证与订单确认邮件。

Order by email and pay via WeChat Pay or Alipay; we issue the key manually. As an individual developer we cannot issue a VAT special invoice, but we provide a payment receipt and order confirmation by email.

以后会涨价吗？Will prices go up?

可能会。但调价只影响之后的新订单，已经买下的期限或买断授权不受影响。

Possibly. Any change applies only to new orders; terms and lifetime licences already purchased are unaffected.
